BrainQuery datasets: what the model trains on and what it is tested on

Each row is a cohort. Blocks to the left are scans used for training (labelled train and validation scans plus the self-supervised pool); blocks to the right are scans held out for testing: a training cohort's own test split, or an external cohort the model never trains on. Rows are coloured by modality and target.

training scansheld-out test split (same cohort)external cohort (test only)

Counts are scans: every MRI sequence counts once, so a 4-sequence BraTS case is 4 scans. External sizes are the most scans any of that cohort's benchmark tasks scored; for segmentation-transfer sets (INSTANCE, ISLES 2022, WMH), the number of cases.

Removed from training so they can serve as external tests: ABIDE I, IXI, OASIS-1/-2, Pixar, RSNA Aneurysm, plus every scan that duplicates a benchmark scan. NKI (5,948 scans) and HBN (8,478) are also held out of training but not yet benchmarked, so they are not shown; BrainMetShare (624) is held out and unused.
