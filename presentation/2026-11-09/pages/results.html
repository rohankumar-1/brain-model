Datasets

Counts are scans: every MRI sequence counts once, so a 4-sequence BraTS case is 4 scans. External sizes are the most scans any of that cohort's benchmark tasks scored; for segmentation-transfer sets (INSTANCE, ISLES 2022, WMH), the number of cases.

Removed from training so they can serve as external tests: ABIDE I, IXI, OASIS-1/-2, Pixar, RSNA Aneurysm, plus every scan that duplicates a benchmark scan. NKI (5,948 scans) and HBN (8,478) are also held out of training but not yet benchmarked, so they are not shown; BrainMetShare (624) is held out and unused.

How we evaluate

Frozen benchmark (v3)

The encoder is frozen; a small readout (linear, MLP, spatial or token-conv, pinned per task on validation) is fitted on each task's training split. Small cohorts are cross-validated 5 x 3; scans that duplicate any training scan are removed.

Trained tasks, own heads

The model's own heads on each training cohort's held-out test split: what the multitask model learned directly.

Zero-shot transfer

A head trained on one cohort read unchanged on an external one (CQ500 with the RSNA hemorrhage heads): no labels from the external site.

Random init

The same architecture, untrained, through the same readouts: the floor that shows what training adds.

Single-task, from scratch

One model per task family (ViT, ResNet or conv-stem ViT of the same size, best kept), trained only on that family: the supervised specialist baseline.

Published models and literature

CT-FM, BrainIAC, BrainFM and HLIP run through our frozen benchmark; published results from the papers for the same tasks.

Caveats

  1. Test sets of 40-130 cases carry ±0.05-0.10 AUC intervals; small differences are noise.
  2. Published numbers use their own splits and often more labelled data or several sequences.
  3. The new run changed several things at once (window bank, re-encoded store, batch 12, fresh optimizer); its gains over R16 cannot be attributed to one of them. R16 was stopped before its learning-rate cooldown.
  4. The native-CT twin starts its CT stem from random weights; its CT groups are still catching up and a stem warm-up is planned before judging native geometry.
  5. Single training seed per model.
  6. Brain-volume regression test rows (62 scans) give negative R² for every model, including R14-F in its earlier evaluation: that split is too narrow to measure; read volumes from validation. Tiny test sets (e.g. glioma IDH, 9 cases) are shown but carry no weight.
  7. Paired on the same test scans, over the 48 tasks both runs trained with at least 50 test scans, the new run averages 0.828 against R14-F's 0.814 (17 wins, 6 losses at |Δ| > 0.01). The CT skull / CSF masks were redefined in labels v12, so R14-F is not scored on them.
